Once you've installed the tracking script, our system automatically detects when everything is working correctly.

![Installation verification dashboard](/images/docs/installation-check.jpg)

## Automatic Detection

Hector Analytics automatically detects when your tracking script is properly installed and collecting data. You'll see:

✅ **Installation Status** - Green indicator when the script is detected

✅ **Real-time Data** - Visitor data are now being collected

✅ **Dashboard Active** - Your analytics dashboard is now active

## You can try it out

After installation, you can try it out by visiting your website and checking your dashboard, you should see real-time data appearing.

## Need Help?

If you don't see data appearing in your dashboard after a few minutes, or if you encounter any installation issues:

**📧 Contact Support**  
Email us at [support@hectoranalytics.com](mailto:support@hectoranalytics.com) and we'll help you get set up quickly.

Our support team typically responds within a few hours and can help troubleshoot any installation problems.
